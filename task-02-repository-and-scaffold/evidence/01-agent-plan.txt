3. **Design System Source of Truth**:

   - **Tonal Primitives**: 94 primitive colors across Brand, Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Neutral, Neutral Variant, and Status families.
   - **Semantic Color Roles**: 9 light roles (`Primary`, `On Primary`, `Surface`, `On Surface`, `Error`, `Warning`, `Success`, `Field Outline`, `Focus Indicator`). UI code must use `--color-role-*`; raw hex and primitive colors are prohibited in layout styles.
   - **Typography**: 15 styles structured under 5 roles (Display, Headline, Title, Body, Label) using the **Inter** family with a uniform $1.4\times$ line-height formula and compact letter spacing (-4% on Display Large).
   - **Shadows**: Soft, Medium, and Hard drop shadows tinted with dark brand hue `#0D2C2B` (`primary/10`) rather than neutral black.
4. **Scope Constraint for this Step**:
