# Tee’s Wardrobe Business Brief

Tee’s Wardrobe is a new clothing business selling a mixture of ready-made garments and made-to-order pieces (including kimonos, two-piece sets and bubu gowns). Standard sizing applies to ready-made items, while measurements are taken for made-to-order or customized orders.

The owner currently works alone. Customers are aged 17 and upwards and contact the business through direct messages. Record-keeping is informal, split between a paper notebook and WhatsApp chats, where delivery charges and payment dates are not always recorded consistently. Bank transfers are confirmed manually using the bank app. The earlier payment-confusion story is unconfirmed; Tee is not sure it really happened.

The one thing that must work is establishing the correct remaining balance for each order from its agreed price and confirmed payments. Clothing prices exclude delivery. Delivery prices are quoted based on customer location and agreed before payment; customer collection is also available. Tee’s Wardrobe receives delivery payments directly. The full agreed charge includes clothing plus delivery once quoted. An unquoted delivery charge must remain visibly “awaiting quote”, not zero or free.

Three recurring customer questions are:
- How much is owed, and was payment received?
- Is the outfit ready, and when can it be collected or delivered?
- Can the order be changed or cancelled?

Changes and cancellations are considered case by case, with no set deposit or refund rule yet. Automated cancellations, refunds and bank integration are outside the proposed first version.

The proposed application lets the owner record orders, prices, payments and progress. Tee prefers that future helpers see orders and delivery details, while measurements and payments remain limited to the owner or those who need them; the current demonstration does not implement sign-in or role-based access controls. Customers could view their own order details, but have not been interviewed. Preferences for simpler WhatsApp links and unreliable mobile data are the owner’s guesses, not confirmed needs.

The interface should feel clear and trustworthy, with simple mobile phone use. Tee’s Wardrobe has no logo yet. The brand color is sea green, sampled from the reference swatch as sRGB #32B1AA (replacing an earlier #2E8B57 shade). Cost, complexity and sluggish phone performance are primary rejection criteria.
