## Architectural Context of this Demonstration
1. Prior to this experiment, task-01-design-system/index.html applied `--color-role-primary` across all brand-accented components (the header badge, the header underline, the table balance highlight, and the FAQ card borders).
2. To demonstrate the contrast between a broken hardcoded role and a functioning palette-linked role on a single screen without destroying the baseline design system, this experiment deliberately introduced a secondary role (`--color-role-brand-accent`) pointing to `var(--color-primitive-brand-primary-40)`. The header underline, financial balance highlight, and FAQ borders were pointed to `--color-role-brand-accent`, while the brand title badge remained bound to `--color-role-primary`, which was severed from the palette and hardcoded to raw `#32B1AA`.
3. In `css/tokens.css`, both the brand-source primitive (`--color-primitive-brand-brand-source`) and the palette primitive (`--color-primitive-brand-primary-40`) were explicitly changed during the experiment from `#32b1aaff` to `#7b2cbfff`; changing the source alone did not automatically regenerate or recompute the palette tones.

## Three Required Sentences (Refined for Architectural Precision)
1. For this experiment, we broke the token architecture by hardcoding the primary colour role (`--color-role-primary`) to a raw hex value (`#32B1AA`) rather than referencing its semantic palette alias (`var(--color-primitive-brand-primary-40)`), while simultaneously linking a demonstration accent role (`--color-role-brand-accent`) to the primitive palette.
2. When both the brand-source value and the primary/40 palette value were updated in the stylesheet from sea green to royal purple (`#7B2CBF`), the correctly linked structural accents dynamically inherited the new brand shade, while the broken title badge remained marooned in the stale sea green.
3. This discrepancy demonstrates that token references ensure linked values update synchronously and consistently across an interface; however, while references automate change propagation, they do not by themselves guarantee that every semantic mapping or contrast pairing is architecturally correct.
